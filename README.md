# 🎃 Halloween Town
A custom seasonal instance for Ragnarok Offline, solo or with your party 👻

It's 11:59pm. The portal to Halloween Town closes at midnight. And a little ghost named Casper just found you 👻

## 🕯️ The Story
Blair was a witch. The people of Halloween Town tied her to a stake and burned her alive 🔥
- Her soul never left. It stayed behind, white as ash and twisted with hate, hunting the ones who set her skin on fire 💀
- The woods took her in. The deeper she went into the shadows, the less it mattered who she killed... revenge wasn't enough anymore 🌑
- She destroyed the whole town. The ones she killed became monsters, and the survivors fled to Umbala 🏃
- Casper was the last soul she siphoned. She was too tired to corrupt him, so he came out of it a shadow... but still himself 🖤

Tonight is Halloween night. Blair wants the shadows to swallow all of Midgard, and only a noble warrior of the light can stop her ✨

## 🌕 Choose Your Moon
Talk to **Casper** in Niflheim (185, 190) and pick which moon to fly to on his dusty old broom 🧹
- 🔵 **Blue Moon (90+)**: works on Pre-renewal and Renewal
- 🔴 **Blood Moon (100-199)**: Renewal only
- 🌑 **Eclipse (200+)**: Renewal only

Same story on every moon. The brighter the moon, the angrier the dead 😈
Higher levels can pick a lower moon too!

## 🏚️ Welcome to Halloween Town (or what's left of it)
A haunted, night-time Niflheim in ruins 🌙
- You land on a moonlit hill, locked in by a ring of jack-o'-lanterns 🎃 Talk to Casper to set them loose
- The sky portal is a safe zone 🛡️ while the dead roam, no monster can stay near it, so you can come back after dying without getting swarmed
- Pumpkins, coffins, candles, skull piles, lanterns and scarecrows all over town 🕸️
- Bats flapping across the night sky 🦇
- Its own spooky soundtrack 🎵
- Clickable navigation links in the dialogue always point you to the next spot 🧭 (no coordinates to type!)

## 🍬 Trick or Treat
- 🎃 **Jack-o'-Champions**: every horde is led by 6 pumpkin-crowned champions (2 of each monster), bigger and 6x as hardy. Each one counts for **10 kills** and pays **everyone** 10 Halloween Coins
- 👻 **The Lost Children**: six little ghosts never found their way home, 2 in each horde. Casper hears them crying and gives you a navigation link 🧭 Find them within 4 minutes, or the shadows take them. Each one found sweetens the party (Blessing and Increase Agility, Lv 10) and pays everyone 10 coins; **all six** bring something special to Blair's hoard 💜
- 🍭 **Candy bowls**: 8 bowls sit on doorsteps around town, somewhere new every night. One knock each: a treat (candy for the party, coins, healing)... or a trick (a Jack-o'-Champion jumps out, or the pumpkin explodes in your face) 💥
- 🕯️ **The Lanterns of Light**: Blair's true form is shielded by her shadows. Stand still beside the 4 jack-o'-lanterns around the portal until they catch fire, and the light burns her shield away for 30 seconds. Then the shadows snuff them out, and it's time to light them again 🔥
- 🏅 **Your night's grade**: time and falls make an **S** (no falls, under 35 minutes), **A** (2 falls at most, under 50), **B** (under 70) or **C**, and the better the grade, the bigger the hoard
- 🏆 **The fastest nights**: Casper in Niflheim remembers the fastest night under each moon (and yours)

## ⚔️ The Night, Step by Step
1. 👻 **Horde 1**: the lost souls Blair is raising in the cemetery
2. 🧙‍♀️ **Blair**: she calls you a grain of stardust... then sends her first MVP
3. 🏠 **Casper's house**: he tells you how his mother died trying to save him. *Every child deserves to go trick or treating!* 🍬
4. 💀 **Horde 2**: more of her dead
5. 🗡️ **Second MVP**: guarding the way to the old hill, with the dead of Hordes 1 and 2 roaming the road
6. 😈 **Blair, again**: she laughs, and the woods answer her call
7. 🌲 **Horde 3**: the biggest one yet
8. 🌀 **The sky portal**: Blair's trying to escape into Midgard. She gets MAD... and transforms 😱
9. ⚰️ **Final MVP**: her true form
10. 🤍 **Blair's cleansed soul**: she thanks you, and finally rests in peace
11. 🎁 **Blair's hoard**: then Casper flies everyone home to Niflheim

## 👥 Bring Your Party!
- The party leader opens the portal, and everyone else just talks to Casper to follow 🧹
- Only the leader talks to the NPCs inside, so the story stays together 🗣️
- **Everyone's** kills count toward the hordes ⚔️
- **Everyone** on the map gets 1 Halloween Coin per kill and 15 per MVP, no matter who landed the hit 🪙
- **Everyone** gets the reward box too 🎁

## 🎃 The Halloween Exchanger
Jakk (Niflheim 180, 191) trades your Halloween Coins in a proper shop window 🛒
- 🥚 Halloween Spooky Pack: 50 coins
- 📦 Halloween Box: 100 coins
- 📜 October Spooky Crate and October Spooky Box: 50 coins each
- 🎩 Costume Halloween Hat, Witch's Hat, Broom of Witch, Halloween Cat Short, Halloween Poring Bag, Sakura Witch Hat and Jakk: 1,500 coins each
- 🃏 Sealed Card Album II: 150,000 coins

On Pre-renewal, any prize the server doesn't have is swapped for a Halloween stand-in automatically 🔄

🎟️ **Halloween Tickets for Cash Points!** Bring Jakk the tickets Blair drops and he'll trade them at **3 tickets = 1 Cash Point** 💎

## ⏳ The Rules
- 90 minutes to stop Blair before the portal closes ⌛
- 8-hour cooldown per character, starting when you enter 🔒
- Casper, Blair and Jakk each have their own portrait pop-ups 🖼️

That's it! Grab your broom, pick your moon, and don't let Blair get through that portal 🧹🌙
Happy Halloween 💜🎃

## 💾 Installing
In Ragnarok Offline: **Settings → Mods → Find Mods → halloween-town → Install**. Works in both eras.
When a new release is out, the Mods tab shows **Update available** 🔔

You can also download `halloween-town-v<version>.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page and install it with **Install from a folder…**

## 🚀 Releases
- ✅ Every pull request runs [validate.yml](.github/workflows/validate.yml): `mod.json`, every `.yml` with rapidyaml, the CHANGELOG, the release zip's layout, and the scripts on an rAthena map-server `--run-once`, in both eras.
- 🏷️ Publishing a GitHub Release tagged `v<version>` builds `halloween-town-v<version>.zip` ([release.yml](.github/workflows/release.yml)) and attaches it. What changed is in [CHANGELOG.md](halloween-town/CHANGELOG.md).
